Reducing medicine waste, saving lives.

Give near-expiry medicine a second life

Pharmacies, hospitals and clinics often hold stocks that will expire soon — while other clinics and NGOs urgently need them. MediShare connects the two: clinics list their near-expiry medicine, the platform matches it with those who need it, and the admin makes sure every donation is safe, approved and delivered. Less waste, more healing.

How it works

Clinics list near-expiry stock

A clinic signs in and donates medicine batches that would otherwise expire on the shelf.

Admin approves & matches
The admin verifies each batch, adds it to shared stock and fulfills requests from clinics that need it — nearest expiry first.
Medicine reaches those in need
Requested medicine is dispensed to NGOs, rural clinics and low-stock health centres before it ever expires.
